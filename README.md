

My Personal Page
=======================================================================================================================================
Imagine that you could know someone just in one click? Well, you can know me by having a look inside this website.

> Test the site just [here](https://alan-personal-page.vercel.app)!

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to run the site
=======================================================================================================================================
Click on the link above. If you're running on a mobile device,  put your device orientation on  _landscape_ and your browser on _Desktop mode_ for an optimal experience.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Features
=======================================================================================================================================
- The home page has a button to click to change the color theme of the page,
- The site features 4 sections: "Home", "Projects", "What's up" and "Facts" that help to know distinct sections of my life,
- The text in the facts and whats'up page is updated every week to keep up with current information, 
- The site has link  to direct users to a way of communication such as an Email, LinkedIn and Github link.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why this theme ?
=======================================================================================================================================
All the information in here is direct, except one that requires a subtile view of the world and an open-minded perpective. The choices of a theme tells long on the person that did the site. The choice of a Spiderman photo rather than. Some say it's unprofessional, I say it's special. With that profile picture, you get to know me even before reading the information in here.

It's a direct expression that our websites are losing this subtile way of seeing, reading in between the lines and even reading the lines. Few are those who will read this sentence
